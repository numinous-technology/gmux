== why the masks look like this ==
Whole card: [10,9,10,9] [9,10,10,9] ... (38 per chiplet, min 9 per engine).
ROCm deals mask bits round robin: bit i -> chiplet i mod 8, then round robin
over that chiplet's engines. The hardware splits a kernel evenly across every
engine a job has units on, so a job runs at the speed of its thinnest engine.

Earlier masks, measured on the same card:
contiguous quarters (bits 0-75, 76-151, ...):
  alone 220.1 221.8 221.1 124.8; four at once 109.7 105.0 109.7 56.8
  (quarter 4 left one engine with a single unit on two chiplets)
bit-striped quarters (bit u%4==k): ignored by the runtime; every job ran on
  all 304 units, so it was timesharing, not partitioning.
